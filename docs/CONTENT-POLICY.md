# Content policy

This repository is public. Everything committed to it is published to the world.

1. Only SRD 5.1 data and my own original work ship. The SRD 5.1 is used under CC-BY-4.0; see [NOTICE](../NOTICE).
2. No book text. Nothing is copied or closely paraphrased from rulebooks.
3. Short mechanical summaries written in my own words, with a page pointer to the source, are allowed.
4. Non-SRD content (the Circle of the Moon druid subclass and the Artillerist artificer subclass) ships only as mechanics-as-data plus my own-words summaries.
5. A user-supplied import for full text may come later. Users would bring their own copy; it would stay on their device and never be committed here.
6. No real player or campaign data in fixtures or anywhere else in the repo. Fixtures use invented characters.
7. No trademarks as product names. The app's name is lanternbook. Saying a work is "compatible with fifth edition" is permitted by the SRD terms; no other Wizards attribution is added.
