# Vision

## Guiding question

Can someone who is not a developer build and maintain something real, safely, without ever having to choose safety?

lanternbook is both the experiment (the method) and the successor to dnd-command-hub (the product); dnd-command-hub stays the table app until parity.

## Principles

- **The right way is the lazy way.** Safety arrives as convenience: the easiest path is the safe one. It is never delivered as advice the person has to remember and follow.
- **Rules live in the agent, not the person.** The checks, conventions and guardrails are carried by the tooling and the agent, so nobody has to learn them to benefit from them.
- **Invisible by default, inspectable on demand.** Nothing about the machinery gets in the way, but anyone who asks can see what happened and why.
- **A stable core plus a sandboxed extension layer with one-tap undo.** Changes people ask for land in the extension layer, cannot break the core, and can be reverted in one step.

## Constraints

Time is very limited. Work comes in small, finishable slices, each with a visible "done" every few weeks.

## Non-goals for now

- Becoming a general platform.
